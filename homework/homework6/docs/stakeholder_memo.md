# Stakeholder Memo

## Stakeholder
Portfolio Manager

## Primary Decision
Determine whether to continue, reduce, or stop capital allocation
to the ETF momentum strategy over the next 12 months.

## Goals
The portfolio manager wants to identify whether the strategy is
expected to continue generating excess return relative to the
S&P 500 while maintaining acceptable downside risk.

## Information Needed
- Expected 12-month return
- Expected excess return relative to benchmark
- Volatility
- Maximum drawdown
- Forecast uncertainty

## Key Concerns
- Changing market regimes
- Transaction costs
- Liquidity
- Strategy capacity
- Model uncertainty

## Desired Deliverable
A concise forecast and risk report that supports the capital
allocation decision.
